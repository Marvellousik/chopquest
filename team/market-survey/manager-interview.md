# Cafeteria manager interview

Keep it to 10 minutes. Be honest: say it's a student hackathon project and nothing is being sold to them.

## Opening (Friday, while collecting the menu)

1. What are your 5 best-selling dishes?
2. What protein do you use most (chicken, beef, fish, egg)? Where do you buy it, and roughly what do you pay per kg?
3. Have meat prices made you raise prices or shrink portions this year?
4. Do you serve anything with beans, soy, moi moi or awara as the main protein? How does it sell?
5. Would a cheaper protein that students still like be useful to you? Why or why not?

## Showing swaps (Saturday morning, with the app open)

Show 3 to 5 swaps for **their own dishes**. For each, record:

## The ask

> "Would you be willing to try one of these as a special for a week?"

If yes: get their name, a signature or a photo with the screen, and the dish they'd try. This is our strongest pitch evidence.

## Consent

Ask before quoting them by name or photographing them. If they say no, record the answer as "a campus cafeteria manager".
