# Cafeteria survey, 20 Nov 2026 (Jason)

Real field notes from the campus cafeterias (7 surveyed in person, 2 more from menu photos). Kept as logged. Gaps are marked; nothing below is estimated.
Menus from the photo set are transcribed into `cafeteria-menus.csv` (prices exactly as printed; blank where the board has none).

## 1. Ase Cafe
- **Menu format:** verbal only (no menu board). Food-warmer counter photo: `d00e286c-5e6a-492c-8779-77b2ebe2f192.jpg`.
- **Top 5 best sellers:** meat pie (beef / minced meat); sausage roll (sausage / meat); white rice (with beef or chicken); fish (staff: most people buy fish); chicken.
- **Dishes:** white rice, beef stew, vegetable soup, fried plantain (dodo), peppered fried fish, meat pie, sausage roll, chicken. Student prices not recorded yet.
- **Meat cost / supplier:** worker said "309" (unit not verified); supplier not disclosed (open market).
- **Plant-protein note:** staff answered "fish" (didn't separate plant from animal protein).

## 2. First Cafe
- **Top 5:** jollof rice; jollof spaghetti; fried rice; Chinese basmati rice; yam and egg sauce (egg).
- **Protein prices (students):** chicken ₦1,500–₦2,000 (biggest piece ₦2,500); beef ₦500; fish ₦500 or ₦1,000; sausage ₦300.
- **Meat cost / supplier:** not logged.
- **Plant-protein note:** **awara** is the vegetarian option most people go for.

## 3. Second Cafe
- **Top 5:** zobo drink + rice and stew; porridge beans (beans); vegetable and egusi soup (egusi); jollof spaghetti; white rice with banga stew.
- **Protein prices:** chicken ₦1,500–₦2,500; beef ₦300; fish ₦1,000–₦2,000.
- **Also on the menu:** white rice and moi moi with tomato stew. Saturday specials: akara and pap; spaghetti with boiled egg.
- **Meat cost / supplier:** not logged.
- **Plant-protein note:** already serves plant pairings: porridge beans, egusi soup, white rice + moi moi, Saturday akara + pap.

## 4. Third Cafe
- **Top 5:** stir-fry pasta; Chinese rice; white rice and chicken curry sauce (chicken); smoky jollof; French fries.
- **Protein prices:** chicken ₦1,500–₦3,000; beef ₦200, ₦300, ₦500; fish not normally sold (when available ₦500 or ₦1,000).
- **Meat cost / supplier:** not logged.
- **Plant-protein note:** **moi moi** is the best plant protein to pair with rice.

## 5. Bush Cafe
- **Top 5:** fried rice; jollof rice; jollof spaghetti; white rice; masa.
- **Protein prices:** chicken ₦1,500, ₦2,000, ₦2,500; beef ₦300; sausage ₦300 (out of stock on the day).
- **Meat cost / supplier:** not logged.
- **Plant-protein note:** **moi moi** is the best plant protein option.

## 6. GG's
- **Top 5:** white rice and stew; jollof rice; fried rice; jollof spaghetti; puff puff.
- **Protein prices:** chicken ₦2,000, ₦2,500, ₦3,000; beef ₦200; fish ₦1,000, ₦1,500.
- **Meat cost / supplier:** not logged.
- **Plant-protein note:** **beans** (with salad) is the best plant pairing.

## 7. Munchbox
- **Top 5:** shawarma (chicken / beef / sausage); special basmati rice; jollof and fried rice (add-ons: plantain, beef, boiled egg, sausage, chicken); pasta; loaded fries.
- **Protein prices:** chicken ₦2,500, ₦3,000, ₦3,500; beef ₦300; fish ₦1,000. Other add-ons: boiled egg, sausage, plantain.
- **Meat cost / supplier:** not disclosed (staff quoted student selling prices).
- **Plant-protein note:** all current proteins are animal-based; the only plant add-on today is plantain.

## Across all 7 surveyed
- **Most popular dishes:** jollof rice, fried rice, jollof spaghetti, white rice and stew, basmati / Chinese rice.
- **Student protein price ranges:** chicken ₦1,500–₦3,500; beef ₦200–₦500; fish ₦500–₦2,000; sausage ₦300.
- **Plant upgrades staff recommend:** moi moi (Third Cafe, Bush Cafe, Second Cafe), beans (GG's, Second Cafe), awara (First Cafe), akara / egusi (Second Cafe).
