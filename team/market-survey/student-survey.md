# Student quick survey

For 10 to 15 students, Saturday morning. Two minutes each. Ask them to leave their rating as a real review in the app (Board, open the dish, then "Write a review"). Record the extra answers below.

## Questions

1. How often do you eat at a campus cafeteria? (daily / few times a week / rarely)
2. Have you eaten soy chunks, awara or moi moi as the main protein in a meal? (yes / no)
3. Looking at this dish [show the swapped dish card]: would you order it at ₦[price]? (yes / maybe / no)
4. What would make you try it? (cheaper / friend recommends / good reviews / tastes like the original / nothing)
5. Would reading other students' reviews make you more likely to try a new dish? (yes / no)

## Rules

- No names needed; hall or department only.
- Never fill in answers for anyone, and never write reviews yourself. Every review on the board must come from a real student.
- Q5 is our evidence that the review board matters. Report the yes percentage.
